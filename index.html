The Platform

Built for the way physicians work.

ProLytix Solutions develops the IQ Suite — a family of discrete, licensable clinical intelligence modules — and Velox, the integration shell that brings them to the point of care. Every output is traceable to a clinical guideline. No black boxes. No cloud exposure. No PHI leaving the building.

Architecture Principles
  • On-premise Rocky Linux edge device — no cloud, no internet required
  • FHIR R4 EHR integration, Epic-compatible from day one
  • Deterministic scoring — every decision traces to a named guideline
  • Powered by Arcos — a proprietary agent orchestration framework
  • Clinic-configurable thresholds via Preferences UI
  • 21st Century Cures Act clinical decision support exemption
  • HIPAA-safe by design — PHI never leaves the facility
Deterministic Intelligence

Every scoring decision is traceable to a specific guideline step in plain language. The MA can read it. The physician can trust it. The attorney can defend it.

On-Premise Edge

Velox runs on a dedicated Rocky Linux device inside the facility. No cloud subscription. No data leaving your building. Full function with no internet connection.

Clinic-Configurable

Your thresholds, your rules, your practice's preferences — not just guideline defaults. Encoded in the Preferences UI. One clinic's protocol doesn't bleed into another's.
