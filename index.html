How We Build

Built for the way your team works.

Across every vertical, ProLytix develops discrete, licensable intelligence modules and pairs them with an integration shell that brings them to the point of use. Every output is traceable to a defined source — a guideline, a rule, a rate table. No black boxes. No cloud exposure required. Nothing sensitive leaving the building unless you choose otherwise.

Architecture Principles
  • On-premise deployment available — no cloud, no internet required
  • Deterministic logic — every output traces to a named source
  • Powered by Arcos — a proprietary agent orchestration framework
  • Configurable thresholds and rules, not just system defaults
  • A qualified person reviews and makes the final call, always
