What It Does

Deadlines and documents,

Special education coordinators track dozens of students at once, each with their own evaluation timeline and documentation requirements. We track every IEP against its required timeline and surface what's missing, before it becomes a compliance finding or a child falls through the cracks.

Architecture Principles
  • Timeline tracking for every required evaluation and review date
  • Missing-documentation detection, same logic as a prior authorization check
  • Deployable on district infrastructure, no cloud subscription required
  • Coordinators and case managers review and act on every flag
