The Problem

Caseloads grow.
Deadlines don't move.

Special education coordinators and case managers are tracking dozens of students at once, each with their own evaluation timeline, service minutes, and re-certification date. When that tracking lives in spreadsheets and memory, deadlines get missed — and a missed IEP deadline is a compliance finding, not just an inconvenience.

What The Platform Does
  • Tracks every IEP against its required timeline — evaluation, annual review, re-evaluation
  • Surfaces upcoming and overdue deadlines before they become findings
  • Keeps documentation requirements visible per student, per requirement
  • Same on-premise, no-cloud-required architecture as our healthcare platform
  • Coordinators and case managers review and act — the platform tracks, it does not decide on a child's behalf
What's Different
