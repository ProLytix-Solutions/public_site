ProLytix Solutions is a small, senior team building guideline-driven intelligence software across healthcare, government, and education. We're looking for people who want to own real problems, not just tickets.

Open Roles

Two positions.
Both foundational.

We're a small team by design. Both roles below report directly into the founders and shape how the company operates, not just what it ships.

Software Engineer / Programmer
Full-Time · Remote-Friendly

You'll work across our product lines — clinical intelligence, tax and revenue intelligence, and custom Arcos-powered applications — building deterministic, guideline-driven systems that real practitioners rely on. This is not a "move fast and break things" role. It's a "get the logic right, every time" role.

  • Strong fundamentals in at least one of Python, C#, or C/C++; comfort picking up others as needed
  • Experience building and shipping production software, not just prototypes
  • Comfort with edge/on-premise deployment constraints — this isn't cloud-only work
  • Careful, methodical approach to logic that has real-world consequences if it's wrong
  • Interest in agentic workflows and orchestration frameworks is a strong plus
Business Development
Full-Time · Remote-Friendly

You'll build and manage the pipeline across our verticals — identifying the right partners, running the outreach, and shepherding relationships from first conversation to signed agreement. Healthcare experience is a strong plus given our current focus, but the core skill is relationship-building and deal execution.

  • Track record of building a pipeline and closing deals, ideally in healthcare, government, or education markets
  • Comfortable being the face of a small company — you're building trust, not reading a script
  • Able to translate technical capability into a pitch a non-technical buyer understands
  • Organized enough to manage a multi-vertical pipeline without things falling through the cracks
  • Genuinely curious about the problems our partners face, not just the deal at the end
