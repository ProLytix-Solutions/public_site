What It Does

Streamlines the day.
Screens what matters.

Our technology monitors the schedule for patients who need to be seen sooner than planned, surfaces the ones who need real prep before their visit, and greenlights the ones who are good to go. It screens incoming referrals programmatically and checks for missing orders before anyone opens a chart.

Velox

Guideline-driven referral and pathway scoring. Screens referrals, flags missing orders, and prioritizes the census so your team focuses time where it's needed.

Navigator

Guideline-driven navigation for the nurse navigator. Presents only the actions that actually need taking, no sorting through a full patient list to find them.

Your Rules, Not Ours

Every pathway is tunable to physician preference. Referrals customized to what you need. Prior authorizations written the way you already know you need them.

How It Fits

Deployed into
what you already run.

Everything runs through REST APIs and can be mapped into any EHR. It can be deployed as a virtual machine or installed on a small, disconnected device that runs fully offline, suited to rural sites or care settings without reliable connectivity. No cloud required. Every scoring step is traceable back to a named guideline for the clinician to review.

Architecture Principles
  • Deterministic scoring, every output traces to a named guideline
  • Dark-site capable, no internet connection required
  • Deployable as a virtual machine or a dedicated edge device
  • REST API integration, maps into any EHR
  • On-site LLM augmentation available, no cloud, you own the data
  • A licensed clinician reviews and makes the final call, always
