Architecture Principles
  • On-premise edge device — no cloud, no internet required
  • FHIR R4 EHR integration, compatible from day one
  • Deterministic scoring — every output traces to a named guideline
  • Powered by Arcos — a proprietary agent orchestration framework
  • Clinic-configurable thresholds via Preferences UI
  • 21st Century Cures Act clinical decision support exemption
  • A licensed clinician reviews and makes the call — always
