Revenue offices classify business activity and calculate tax and license fees against rate tables that change by jurisdiction and by year. We turn those documents and tables into a calculator that applies the current rule automatically, and shows the reasoning behind every figure it produces.

Architecture Principles
  • Deterministic classification and calculation, traceable to a named rule
  • Built directly from your existing tax documents and tables
  • On-site LLM support for year-over-year trend mapping and analysis
  • No cloud exposure, you own the full data stream
  • A revenue officer reviews and finalizes every result
